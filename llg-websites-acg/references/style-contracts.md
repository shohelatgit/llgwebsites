# First-five visual contracts

These contracts were derived from the local clone mirrors. Confidence is high for desktop hierarchy and component anatomy; mobile behavior is implemented from the clone's responsive markup and validated at 390px and 320px.

## Connecticut Drain Pros — HorizonFix

- Full-viewport photographic hero, dark overlay, transparent fixed navigation, centered message, paired solid/outline CTAs, and a scroll cue.
- Clean utility palette: black, white, slate surfaces, blue action color; rounded medium buttons and simple line icons.
- Three-up service cards and a dark proof/CTA band; mobile stacks the hero actions and cards.

## Dallas Drain Guys — Minuteman

- High-contrast service-company shell: compact notice bar, roomy header, confident editorial headline, wide image block, and prominent red action buttons.
- Alternating white, pale gray, and deep navy bands; square-to-soft card corners, dense service grid, and large footer conversion panel.
- Mobile keeps the phone action visible and converts multi-column blocks to a single reading column.

## Louisville Precision Walls — Pink's Concrete

- Centered, crisp Montserrat-style type; warm neutral concrete surfaces; rounded display cards; strong orange/red accent action.
- Hero proof list, six-card service gallery, before/after-style media rhythm, and large review proof strip.
- Mobile reduces display scale without hiding proof or primary contact action.

## Morgantown Fence Pros — Welborn Garage

- Bold, high-energy service layout with a persistent utility phone action, oversized headline, yellow action treatment, and strong image-first sections.
- Full-width proof bands, punchy outlined service modules, testimonial cards, and large bottom quote panel.
- Mobile collapses navigation into a real disclosure control and preserves one-tap call/quote access.

## Pittsburgh French Drain — Roof Right Now

- Ubuntu-style type, saturated blue/white surfaces with a red action color, broad hero copy next to an always-visible estimate card.
- Service pills, warranty/trust band, method/process sections, and direct-response CTA repetition.
- The estimate card drops below hero copy on narrow viewports while retaining form labels and consent copy.
