FAQ's ・ WHAT CLIENTS ASK US

Frequently Asked Questions

Answering your questions about roofing.

The lifespan of a roof depends on the material used:
- Asphalt Shingles: Typically last 12 to 30 years.
- Metal Roofs: Can endure 30 to 50 years.
- Slate Roofs: Often last 75 to 150 years.
- Clay Tiles: May last over a century. Regular maintenance can extend these durations.

Key signs include:
- Age of roof over 20 years
- Curling or missing shingles
- Leaks or water damage
- Sagging roof deck
- Light showing through roof boards
- Higher energy bills

Most residential roof replacements take 1-3 days. Factors affecting timeline:
- Home size
- Roof complexity
- Weather conditions
- Material type
Larger or more complex projects may take longer.

While possible, we recommend complete removal of old shingles. This allows inspection of the deck, ensures proper installation, and maximizes roof lifespan. Most building codes limit re-roofing to two layers.

To prepare:
- Remove loose items from attic
- Clear driveway and yard of vehicles and furniture
- Secure outdoor decorations
- Protect landscaping
- Make arrangements for pets
We'll provide detailed preparation instructions before the project begins.
